# INV-009 — Revenue and operating costs

Updated: 2026-10-14 (Asia/Calcutta). State: **Queued**. Depends on: INV-008 closed. Research owner: Codex; decision owner: product owner. [Tracker](../product-plan.md).

## Decision to establish

How to package the accepted paid AI companion (DEC-017), with exact free/paid boundaries, pricing experiments and sustainable research/data/model service limits. Private development precedes public charging (DEC-016).

## Research questions

- Use actual participant evidence from INV-001 and accepted capabilities to define the paid contextual research/planning value. The paid-AI direction is accepted; specific entitlements and demonstrated value are not. Treat INR 149/month or INR 1,499/year as untested candidates; stated enthusiasm is not payment evidence.
- DEC-012 requires PV1 external validation before public launch. Define real payment/usage evidence for independent beta users, preserve founder-first discovery limitations, and send those findings back to reopened INV-001. No external interview pool exists yet.
- Compare current competitor packaging from primary sources, distinguishing free tracking from paid analysis/advice and term/taxes/introductory prices.
- Include ongoing context updates, source/news/fundamental rights, deep research/tool calls, inference/caching and failed/repeated research in costs; estimate quality/cost tradeoffs rather than treating each AI answer as a single cheap call.
- Build low/base/high cost scenarios with provider minimum commitments and per-account/per-refresh costs, quote licences, AI, infrastructure, verification, support, payments/store fees and acquisition.
- Separate fixed costs from variable cost per active/free/paid user. Calculate contribution margin and conversion/retention requirements; unknown fees remain unknown, not zero.
- Decide refresh/AI allowances, trials, cancellations/refunds, exports and treatment of disconnected accounts. Make limits understandable and align them with the intended recurring benefit.
- Compare subscription with other revenue approaches and their effect on trust and INV-002 eligibility. No advertising, commissions, data sale or lifetime tier is accepted by the current business direction.

## Evidence required

Dated official pricing/quotes, usage assumptions with sensitivity analysis, audience payment evidence and an approved experiment. Research purchases and recurring commitments require a separate cost decision under DEC-004.

## Experiment to define

Specify the offer, honest supported capabilities, audience, actual payment or other behavioural measure, refund/cancellation treatment and success/failure decision. A paid experiment starts only when a deliverable service and applicable requirements exist; no money is collected during tracker setup.

## Closure and next action

Close with packaging and pricing-experiment decisions, service limits, cost ceiling and sustainable-margin criteria or explicit business-model reconsideration. Do not treat a subscriber multiplication example as a forecast.
