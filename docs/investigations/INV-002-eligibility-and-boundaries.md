## Findings and application to Corpus

**Source-grounded:** securities-related advice includes portfolio advice and financial planning; public/general-comment provisions have conditions. Registration, AI responsibility/disclosure and risk/suitability obligations matter for an adviser (E201/E202). These sources establish neither that every budget calculation is regulated nor that an educational label exempts an app.

**Application inference:** T001 can mix cash-flow arithmetic and investment decisions. Comparing user-chosen scenarios differs from directing a person to pause securities contributions, sell assets or adopt an allocation. Review actual outputs/marketing; omission of a stock name is not proof of exemption. A partner must genuinely supply the regulated service and fulfil its obligations, not merely lend a name or API key.

**AA finding:** a Financial Information User (FIU) is regulated; operating an Account Aggregator (AA) is a separate regulated role. A technical supplier does not become an FIU by supplying software. Purpose, authorised recipient and onward use require a real permitted arrangement (E203/E204).

**Implications:** the owner's successful INDmoney connection is not Corpus permission. Provider-specific access remains for INV-003–INV-007. Manual upload changes acquisition, not all advice/privacy duties. Mixed-asset outputs involving deposits, EPF, insurance or foreign securities need their own scope/jurisdiction review; do not universalise SEBI securities rules. Privacy commencement is phased and the launch date is unknown: refresh requirements before launch (E206/E207).
